About Me

Working toward human-centered healthcare at the intersection of medicine, AI, and the humanities.

I am a medical student at Nara Medical University with a Master's degree in Transcultural Studies jointly awarded by Heidelberg University and Kyoto University. My interests lie at the intersection of medicine (especially psychiatry), AI / data science, and the humanities.

Alongside my medical studies, I work as a data scientist and lecturer / teaching assistant at the Matsuo Institute and the University of Tokyo Matsuo Laboratory, and as a research assistant at the Department of Psychiatry, Nara Medical University, where I develop LLM-based tools for psychotherapy training and medical text structuring. From October 2026, I will join the Department of Psychiatry and Neurosciences at Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin as a visiting research intern.

I aim to contribute to the realization of human-centered healthcare by combining the insights and values of the humanities with the power of AI and data-driven technologies.

Research Interests

My current work centers on applying large language models to psychiatry and mental healthcare — from training tools for psychotherapy to safety evaluation of AI systems in clinical contexts.
