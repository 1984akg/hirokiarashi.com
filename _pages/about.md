I am a medical student at Nara Medical University and a data scientist. My work sits at the intersection of **psychiatry**, **AI and natural language processing**, and **the humanities**.

Before medicine, I studied French literature at the University of Tokyo, completed a joint Master's degree in Transcultural Studies at Kyoto University and Heidelberg University, and worked at the Japan Foundation. I entered medical school in 2023 because I wanted to return to the sciences and to build a profession in which expertise, clinical practice, and research go together.

Within medicine, I work on psychiatry and language AI. Psychiatry is the field in which language is the primary clinical instrument—symptoms are expressed, understood, and treated through words—which makes it a natural place to apply language technology. My current research includes an LLM-based training system for motivational interviewing and safety benchmarks for LLMs in mental health care. Alongside this, I work on applied AI at Matsuo Institute, from enterprise knowledge platforms and AI agents to the security of LLM agents.

My training in literature and cultural studies has turned out to be a direct asset in this work: it taught me to attend to what is said, what is left unsaid, and how culture shapes both. Through this combination, I hope to contribute to **human-centered healthcare**.
